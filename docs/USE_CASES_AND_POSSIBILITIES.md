  - Zero SaaS licensing fees, zero third-party cloud dependencies, and complete data sovereignty behind corporate firewalls.
* ⚡ **Sub-Second Inter-Departmental Settlement**:
  - Multi-subsidiary conglomerates can reconcile inter-company balances, invoices, and transfers at 1 block per second with continuous topological DAG ordering.
* 🔐 **Tamper-Proof Audit & Compliance Logging**:

  - Even if internal SQL/ERP databases are breached or altered, mathematical proofs anchored to GhostDAG UTXO commitments prove the exact state and timestamp of original records.
