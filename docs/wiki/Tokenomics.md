# Zyanya Tokenomics ($ZYN)

The monetary policy and token economics of the **Zyanya ($ZYN)** blockchain are engineered to enforce radical fairness, eliminate founder extractivism, align long-term miner incentives, and provide native economic yield through autonomous decentralized exchange (DEX) activity.

---

### 1. Fundamental Asset Metrics

- 🪙 **Asset Ticker**: `$ZYN` (Zyanya)
- 🔬 **Atomic Unit**: `sompi` ($1 \text{ ZYAN} = 100,000,000 \text{ sompi} = 10^8 \text{ sompi}$)
- ⏱️ **Block Velocity**: 1 Block Per Second (1 BPS) parallel GhostDAG
- ⛏️ **Proof-of-Work Algorithm**: AstroBWTv3 (memory-hard, branch-heavy CPU mining)
- 🚫 **Premine**: Exactly **Zero (0 ZYAN)**
- 🔒 **Genesis Distribution**: Genesis block outputs 50 ZYAN to an unspendable `OP_FALSE` script
- 📉 **Emission Decay**: Deflationary smooth-curve reduction (smooth halving cycle)

---

### 2. Coinbase Emission & Miner Vesting

Unlike traditional proof-of-work chains where miners dump 100% of newly minted blocks immediately on open markets, Zyanya introduces native on-chain **Dual-Stream Coinbase Vesting**:

- **Total Reward**: **50 ZYAN per block**
- 💧 **Liquid Stream (50% / 25 ZYAN)**:
  • Immediately spendable upon block maturity (10 confirmations)  
  • Covers ongoing operational hardware, electricity, and node hosting expenses  
- ⏳ **Vested Stream (50% / 25 ZYAN)**:
  • Locked directly in a deterministic time-decay UTXO contract  
  • Unlocks linearly across 12 calendar months (~31,536,000 blocks)  
  • Aligns miner incentives with long-term protocol security and network valuation  

---

### 3. Absolute Zero Premine Guarantee

Zyanya was born out of the cypherpunk ethos of pure fair launch:

- **No Venture Capital (VC) Allocation**: Zero private sales, zero seed rounds, zero discounted SAFT agreements.
- **No Team or Foundation Treasury**: No arbitrary founder tokens reserved at genesis.
- **Provable Unspendable Genesis**:
  • The genesis block contains an unspendable standard script:
  ```
  OP_FALSE
  ```
  • The initial 50 ZYAN generated by the genesis transaction can never be spent, moved, or claimed by any cryptographic key.
- **Level Playing Field**: The very first spendable ZYAN is mined on an open, public network through AstroBWTv3 CPU proof-of-work.

---

### 4. Subnetwork 3 Protocol Fees & Sovereign Staking

Zyanya incorporates native stack-based smart contracts on Subnetwork 3. Unlike chains where trading fees are pocketed by centralized liquidity platforms, Zyanya routes protocol fees directly back to sovereign coin holders:

- 🔄 **0.3% Protocol Fee**:
  • Every swap through the native constant-product AMM (`dex.zcl`) or bonding curve (`bonding_curve.zcl`) incurs a standardized 0.3% liquidity fee.
- 🏦 **Staking Vault Distribution (`staking.zcl`)**:
  • Protocol fees are routed to the decentralized staking contract.  
  • Users lock ZYAN in UTXO staking vaults to receive real-yield distributions funded by network volume.  
  • Yield is denominated in native ZYAN and paid proportionally to staked share weight over time.  

---

### 5. Bonding Curve Graduation Mechanics

Tokens launched via Zyanya's autonomous bonding curve follow a strictly deterministic liquidity progression:

- **Continuous Price Discovery**: Initial trading occurs on a mathematically bounded bonding curve ($P = f(R)$).
- **Graduation Threshold**: When the curve accumulates **1,000,000,000 sompi (10 ZYAN)** in reserve:
  • The curve automatically seals further continuous issuance.  
  • Accumulated reserves and minted tokens are migrated atomically to the AMM pool (`dex.zcl`).  
  • A permanent liquidity pair is instantiated with 0% founder access.
